The problem

Organisations adopt AI on anecdote and abandon it on anecdote. Four ways that goes wrong — and what answers each.

  1. 01

    “It felt better.”

    No baseline, no rubric, no before-and-after. When the only evidence is an impression, the first bad week ends the project — and nobody can say whether it was ever working.

    A 320-question harness pointed at your own domain, re-run on every change.

  2. 02

    “It forgot.”

    Every session starts from zero. What the agent worked out yesterday is gone, so the same ground gets re-explained forever and nothing compounds.

    Memory that outlives the session, carried between runs.

  3. 03

    “I can’t defend this.”

    An answer you cannot explain does not survive a room that pushes back. The hard skill is not producing output — it is standing behind it.

    A loop that checks its own work and shows what it checked.

  4. 04

    “Which model, though?”

    Every release resets your judgement, and the comparisons on offer are marketing. Choosing on benchmarks you did not run is guessing with extra steps.

    One bridge, every provider, measured on your work rather than theirs.
